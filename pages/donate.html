Donate Blood

Who Can Donate?

Generally healthy adults meeting age and weight rules. Always confirm eligibility with a qualified donation center.

Before Donation

Eat well, drink water and carry a valid ID.

Donation Process

Registration, health screening, donation and a short rest.

After Donation

Rest, hydrate and follow the center's after-care advice.
